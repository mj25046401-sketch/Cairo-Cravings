Authentic Egyptian Heritage

From the Streets of Cairo to Temple Terrace

Tampa is home to wonderful Mediterranean spots, but the true spirit of Egyptian street food was missing. Cairo Cravings was founded to share the vibrant, plant-rich, high-energy comfort foods of Downtown Cairo and Alexandria.

✔

Real Fava Bean Ta’ameya: Egyptians never use chickpeas for falafel. We use soaked fava beans, fresh dill, and leeks for that signature emerald-green interior.

✔

Stone Hearth Baladi Flatbread: Made fresh daily with whole wheat flour and toasted wheat bran (radda).

✔

The 6-Hour Koshary Salsa: Slow-simmered vine tomatoes, garlic vinegar da’a, and crispy ward onions.
